StudySpace

A local study dashboard that turns your notes into summaries, quizzes and flashcards using free open-source AI models through OpenRouter. It also includes a focus timer, assignment tracker, exam countdowns and a score graph, all in one page.

Everything runs on your own computer. Your files are read locally, and only the extracted text is sent to OpenRouter when you click Generate.

Features
Upload study material: TXT, MD, CSV, PDF (text-based), DOCX and PPTX, by file picker or drag and drop. Several files can be added at once.
AI study kit: one click generates 5 to 8 notes, 5 multiple-choice questions and 6 to 10 flashcards from your document.
Interactive quiz: instant right/wrong feedback with an explanation for each answer.
Flashcards: click a card to flip it, and move forward or back through the deck.
Focus timer: a 25-minute Pomodoro timer with pause, resume and reset.
Assignments and exams: add, complete and delete tasks, and track exam dates with countdowns.
Progress graph: log quiz and test scores and see your improvement over time.
Automatic model fallback: if a free model is busy, slow or missing, the app tries a backup model instead of hanging.
Saved in your browser: tasks, exams, scores and generated study kits persist in localStorage.
